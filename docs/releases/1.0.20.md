# Magpie 1.0.20

Connected assistants can now search and read your clipboard history, including older clips and new copies. Turn on **Allow access to clipboard history** in **Magpie → Settings → MCP**. Access starts off, and Magpie remembers your choice across launches.

Existing connections continue to work after you enable access. You can turn it off in Settings at any time. Deleted clips disappear from subsequent searches.
