# Magpie 1.0.18

Clipboard history now loads in pages as you scroll. Magpie fetches lightweight previews in batches of 60 and loads image thumbnails as their rows appear. Search still covers your entire history, including clips you haven’t scrolled to yet.

Choose **Settings → General → History → Unlimited** to keep more than 1,000 clips. Pinned clips are kept at every history limit.
